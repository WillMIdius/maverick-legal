# Privacy Policy – Maverick Tutor

**Last updated:** 10 December 2025

## 1. Who we are

We are Maverick Education Technologies Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”), a company registered in the United Kingdom.

We provide an AI-powered GCSE maths tutoring app for students and a web dashboard for parents/guardians.

For the purposes of UK data protection law (including UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), we are the data controller of the personal data described in this policy.

- **Company name:** Maverick Education Technologies Ltd  
- **Contact email for privacy matters:** archie@idius.ai

## 2. What data we collect

We collect the following categories of information.

### 2.1 Account information

- Student first name
- Parent/guardian name (if provided)
- Email address (typically parent/guardian email)
- Username
- Encrypted password
- Basic account settings and preferences

### 2.2 Lesson & progress data

During and after lessons we collect:

- Text transcripts of the conversation between the student and the AI tutor  
  - We do not store lesson audio; only the transcript
- Whiteboard content (what the student writes/draws, including recognised text)
- Topics and questions covered in each lesson
- Time, date and duration of lessons
- Progress within topics (for example: started, in progress, completed)
- Performance indicators (for example, question types the student finds difficult)
- Lesson summaries and reports
- Feedback given by the student or parent/guardian (for example, ratings, comments)

### 2.3 Technical information

When you use the app or dashboard we may collect:

- Device information (for example, device type, operating system, app version)
- IP address
- Basic log data (errors, crashes, performance information)

We do not use precise location tracking.

### 2.4 Microphone and network access

To deliver voice-based lessons:

- The app uses the microphone so the student can speak to the tutor.
- The app uses the network connection to send audio to our AI provider and receive responses.

We do not store the raw audio. We only store the text transcripts produced from it.

## 3. How we use your data

We use personal data for the following purposes:

1. **To provide the service**
   - Create and manage accounts
   - Run live voice-based GCSE maths tutoring sessions
   - Track progress through the GCSE maths syllabus
   - Show lesson history and reports to students and parents/guardians
2. **To personalise learning**
   - Address the student by first name
   - Tailor explanations and questions based on previous performance
   - Identify areas where the student may need extra support
3. **To generate reports and insights**
   - Turn lesson transcripts and whiteboard content into structured summaries
   - Provide parents/guardians with an overview of topics covered and progress
   - Support future features such as reviewing past lessons and longer-term insights
4. **To operate, secure and improve the product**
   - Monitor usage and performance
   - Fix bugs and improve reliability
   - Develop new features and learning tools
   - Protect against misuse and security issues
5. **To communicate with parents/guardians**
   - Send essential service messages (for example, account, policy or feature updates)
   - Share lesson reports and progress information
   - Respond to support queries

### Legal bases under UK GDPR

We typically rely on:

- **Performance of a contract** – to provide the service you sign up for
- **Legitimate interests** – to operate, improve and secure the service in a way that does not override your rights and interests

Where we rely on consent (for example, for any optional marketing), you can withdraw it at any time by contacting archie@idius.ai.

## 4. Use of AI models and service providers

To deliver the service, we use third-party providers that process data on our behalf (“processors”).

### 4.1 AI models

We use AI services (including models provided by OpenAI) to:

- Convert spoken audio into text
- Generate AI tutor responses during lessons
- Create lesson summaries, reports and similar educational outputs

In practice:

- During lessons, audio from the student’s microphone is sent securely to our AI provider.
- The provider returns text and/or audio responses.
- We store the text transcripts and related lesson data in our own systems.
- We may send lesson transcripts and progress data to AI models to generate reports and future review tools.

These providers act under our instructions and are bound by contracts that require them to handle data securely and in line with data protection law.

### 4.2 Hosting and infrastructure

We use cloud hosting and database providers (for example, a managed MongoDB service) to store:

- Account information
- Lesson transcripts and whiteboard data
- Progress data and reports
- Technical logs

These providers offer encryption and access controls. We remain responsible for how your data is used.

## 5. Where your data is processed

Our core systems are operated from the UK and/or European Economic Area (EEA).

Some of our processors (including AI providers) may process data outside the UK/EEA.

Where this happens, we use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent measures) as required by UK data protection law.

## 6. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as we reasonably need it for the purposes described in this policy and to meet legal or accounting requirements.

### 6.1 General retention approach

We keep data:

- While your account is active, and
- For a limited period afterwards so that:
  - Students and parents/guardians can review recent lessons and progress
  - We can support and launch features such as reviewing past lessons and longer-term insights

After this period, we delete or anonymise data so it can no longer be linked to an individual.

### 6.2 Specific periods

Current retention periods are:

- **Account and basic billing data**
  - Kept while the account is active and for up to 18 months after the last activity or account closure, unless we are required to keep it longer for legal reasons.
- **Lesson transcripts, whiteboard content, progress data and reports**
  - Kept while the account is active and for up to 18 months after the student’s last lesson.
  - This supports current features (reports, progress tracking) and future features such as reviewing past lessons and long-term progress.
- **Technical logs**
  - Typically kept for up to 90 days for security and diagnostics, then deleted or anonymised.

We may retain anonymised or aggregated data (which no longer identifies an individual) for longer periods to help us analyse and improve the service.

If you ask us to delete data sooner, we will do so where we are not required to keep it for legal or legitimate reasons.

## 7. How we protect your data

We use technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encryption at rest provided by our hosting/database providers
- Secure password storage (hashed and salted, never plain text)
- Access controls so only authorised personnel can access production systems
- Monitoring and logging of our infrastructure

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to minimise risk and respond quickly to issues.

If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and, where required, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

## 8. Children’s data

Our service is designed for GCSE students and their parents/guardians.

We aim to follow the principles of the UK ICO’s Age Appropriate Design Code, including:

- High privacy by default
- Collecting only data needed for tutoring, progress tracking and improvement
- Not using children’s data for behavioural advertising
- Giving parents/guardians visibility into progress and reports
- Encouraging students not to share sensitive personal details (such as full home address or medical information) during lessons

## 9. Your rights and how to exercise them

Under UK data protection law, you (or a parent/guardian acting on behalf of a child) have rights, including:

- Access to your personal data
- Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- Deletion of your data in certain circumstances
- Restriction of how your data is used in some cases
- Data portability in certain cases
- Objection to certain types of processing (including processing based on legitimate interests)
- Withdrawal of consent where we rely on consent

We do not currently provide in-app buttons or automated flows for these rights.

To exercise any of these rights or to ask questions about how we handle data, please contact: **archie@idius.ai**
